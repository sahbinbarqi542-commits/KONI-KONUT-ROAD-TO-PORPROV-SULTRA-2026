## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a dual personality: **Athletic Vitality meets Civic Authority**. Engineered for the administrative backbone and competitive spirit of KONI Konawe Utara heading into Porprov Sultra 2026, the visual language balances high-stakes tournament energy with the structured governance required by regional athletic federations.

### Tone & Personality
- **Triumphant & Driven:** Evoking the prestige of championship medals, peak physical discipline, and provincial honor.
- **Institutional & Trustworthy:** Delivering the clean, verifiable accountability of an official governmental sports commission.
- **Dynamic & Swift:** Providing mobile-first clarity for coaches, field managers, and verification officials working under direct field conditions.

### Design Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **Modern Corporate & Sportive Technical**. It pairs crisp border structures, high-legibility typographic hierarchies, and restrained metric card surfaces with vibrant championship accents (crimson, gold, and maritime coastal blue). The interface avoids frivolous ornamentation in favor of crisp pill badges, precision status indicators, subtle surface elevations, and actionable operational density.

## Colors

The palette establishes clear hierarchical priorities that honor both national athletic tradition and regional geographic heritage:

- **Primary (`#C8102E` / `#9E0D24`):** The signature KONI Crimson Red. Used for principal brand presence, primary call-to-action buttons, key tab highlights, and urgent administrative cues.
- **Secondary (`#FFB81C` / `#D48806`):** Champion Gold. Used to denote medal counts, top-tier targets, podium achievement metrics, and high-visibility badges.
- **Tertiary (`#0F4C81` & `#0D9488`):** Deep Maritime Blue and Emerald Teal, embodying the coastal and natural expanse of North Konawe (Konawe Utara). Applied to analytical charts, structural navigation elements, and secondary category badges.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` on light, `#F8FAFC` on dark):** Deep slate typography that guarantees AA/AAA contrast across all data views and forms.

### Status Semantic Colors
Status badges use dedicated container and content pairings that maintain clarity without visual noise:
- **Verified / Approved:** Background `#DCFCE7` with Text `#166534`.
- **Pending / In Review:** Background `#FEF9C3` with Text `#854D0E`.
- **Rejected / Needs Revision:** Background `#FEE2E2` with Text `#991B1B`.

### Dark Mode Adaptation
Dark mode shifts the canvas to `#0B1120` with surface cards at `#1E293B` and structural dividers at `#334155`. Brand crimson softens slightly in luminous contrast, while champion gold acts as an ambient beacon on metric surfaces.

## Typography

The design system exclusively implements **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project a clean, high-performance, contemporary Indonesian civic feel.

- **Headlines:** Set with extra bold weights (700 and 800) to command authority in tournament dashboards, banner headers, and athlete profile intros. Tight letter-spacing (-0.02em) keeps large titles athletic and punchy.
- **Body:** Uses regular (400) and medium (500) weights with ample line heights to ease long-form table scanning, regulatory guidelines, and multi-field administrative forms.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or semi-bold tracking is applied to sport categories, badge pills, and field verifications to ensure legibility when viewed on smartphone screens in bright stadium outdoor light.
- **Metric Displays:** Specialized large-scale figures (`32px` / `800`) ensure instant recognition for medal tallies, verified quotas, and contingent headcount.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible, mobile-first responsive grid system engineered for field portability:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid with `2rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer page margins. Enables split views with a side navigation rail, sticky metric summary cards, and multi-column verification data tables.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Metric overview cards collapse into 2x2 grids, and verification queues use collapsible drawer views.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Primary views emphasize single-column vertical cards, bottom sticky action sheets for approvals, and horizontally swipeable filter tabs.

Spacing tokens follow a strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm (`0.25rem` to `2rem`), maintaining compact form ergonomics without crowding data inputs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a hybrid of **Tonal Surface Layering** and **Ambient Tinted Shadows**:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Light `#F8FAFC` or Dark `#0B1120`. Completely flat, non-elevated ground plane.
- **Level 1 (Card & Containers):** White `#FFFFFF` or Dark `#1E293B` bounded by a subtle `1px` border (`#E2E8F0` / `#334155`). Supported by an ambient diffused shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Controls):** Elevated with an athletic glow or lift: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Lightboxes & Quick-Previews):** Used for document review overlays and modal verification dialogs: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)`. In dark mode, shadows are bolstered by a 1px border highlight (`#475569`).
- **Medal Cards Distinction:** Special champion metric cards feature an ultra-soft gold ambient reflection (`rgba(255, 184, 28, 0.15)`) along the top perimeter border.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (`2`)** geometry profile:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Standard interactive elements such as form inputs, standard action buttons, dropdowns, and utility alerts.
- **Card Radius (1rem / 16px):** Metric widgets, document upload dropzones, athlete identity cards, and verification panels.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for **Status Badges** (Pending, Diterima, Ditolak), **Medal Counter Chips**, and **Segmented Tab Selectors**. This creates a sharp visual contrast between administrative structural surfaces and sporting operational states.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Crimson Red (`#C8102E`) with white text, font weight 600, rounded 8px. Hover state deepens to `#9E0D24` with a subtle elevation shift. Focus ring features a 2px offset in `#C8102E`.
- **Secondary / Gold:** Filled in Gold (`#FFB81C`) with dark slate text (`#0F172A`) for high-priority tournament milestones or medal submission triggers.
- **Outline / Ghost:** 1px border in `#E2E8F0` (`#334155` dark) with neutral text, shifting to neutral tint on hover.
- **Mobile Touch Target:** Minimum 44px height across all button variants.

### Status Badge Pills
- Rendered with full pill radius (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical padding, and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Include a 6px circular solid dot indicator beside the text label for immediate color and state recognition.
- Text is set to `label-sm` with uppercase tracking.

### Cards & Metrics
- **Metric Card:** Standard 16px radius, containing an icon tile (36x36px with 10% opacity brand background), label in `label-md`, and numeric output in `metric-display`.
- **Athlete Verification Card:** Features a 3x4 photo preview thumbnail, NIK badge, cabor tag, and action drawer for "Quick Preview" and 1-click status validation.

### Inputs & File Upload
- **Form Fields:** 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 8px radius, height 42px, internal padding `space-md`. Focus transitions to 2px primary crimson border.
- **File Upload Dropzone:** Dashed 2px border in `#CBD5E1` (`#475569` dark), background `#F8FAFC` (`#0F172A` dark), featuring instant image thumbnail generation and client-side compression feedback indicators.

### Document Preview Modal
- Full viewport scrim with 40% blur backdrop (`backdrop-blur-sm`).
- Top banner with document metadata, immediate download trigger, and dual approval actions: Green (Setujui) and Red (Tolak dengan Catatan).

### Tabs & Segmented Filters
- Pill-shaped container housing horizontal sliding switches. Selected segment takes solid surface background with subtle elevation, unselected segments remain muted neutral.
